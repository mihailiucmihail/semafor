# Semafor — facts (source of truth for the website texts)
- Shopify app "Semafor: COD Fraud & Recovery" by Kastel Labs. Listing: apps.shopify.com/semafor.
- Every new order gets a traffic light: green / yellow / red, plus a Shopify tag (🔴⚫⚫ ⚫🟡⚫ ⚫⚫🟢) and a Shopify order-risk note, plus an "Semafor" block on the order page.
- Red = customer (e-mail, phone, name+address, address, device) is on the merchant's blacklist, or reported by enough stores in the network. Yellow = reported by fewer stores in the network, an unshipped/in-transit COD order of the same customer, or 3+ identities on one device in 48h, or other orders from the same device.
- Checkout rules (Basic+): separately for red and yellow: only flag, hide cash on delivery only (card still works), or hide every payment method (order cannot be placed). Optional automatic cancellation of red orders. Customer is never told they were blocked.
- Device recognition: a web pixel installed with the app links all checkout attempts from the same browser/device (90 days of orders), even under different names, e-mails, phones. Typing a blacklisted e-mail/phone on a known device is blocked at checkout.
- Store network: stores share only anonymous hashes (HMAC), never names/addresses. A refused parcel is shared from the 2nd case; chargebacks and threats right away. Merchant sets thresholds (e.g. yellow from 1 store, red from 3) and which reasons count.
- Blacklist = "dossier": identities, reason, source, orders, checkout attempts, timeline. CSV import.
- Pop-up (Basic+): welcome discount for new visitors, asks again at checkout; code sent by e-mail and applied automatically in the cart; 5 designs (classic + 4 seasons); 5 storefront languages (ro, de, pl, en, es). Merchant must enable the "Semafor pop-up" app embed in the theme editor (one switch).
- Recovery e-mails (Pro): up to 3 automatic reminders — ~1 hour after leaving; next morning in the customer's time zone with a discount (15% default) unless the cart already has one; day 3 last chance with a bigger discount (20%) valid that day. Stops when the customer orders; skips sold-out items; reaches pop-up subscribers who left before typing an e-mail. 3 designs: Elegant, Minimal, Modern. Sent by Semafor (no mail service needed) or from the merchant's own domain.
- Attribution: orders brought by the pop-up / cart pop-up / reminder e-mail are tagged ("Semafor · Pop-up", "Semafor · E-mail") with revenue stats.
- Plans: Free $0 (50 checked orders / month, blacklist + CSV, network, risk tag). Basic $9.99/mo ($7.99/mo yearly). Pro $24.99/mo ($19.99/mo yearly). Every new store gets 14 days of Pro free, no charge; afterwards it moves to Free unless a plan is chosen. Billing goes through the merchant's Shopify invoice (no separate card to enter).
- Admin languages: English, Romanian, Spanish, French, Polish, Russian.
