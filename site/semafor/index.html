01 · The signal

The checkout changes with the light.

Every new order is matched against your blacklist and the Semafor store network. Then the light decides what that buyer can do at checkout — and they never find out why.

A trusted buyer.

Nothing changes. Every payment method stays available and checkout is as smooth as ever.

  • Prepaid orders aren't held back by another parcel on its way
  • Zero friction for your good customers

Worth a second look.

Semafor tags the order with the reason. For buyers flagged by the store network, you can also set a checkout rule — for example, no cash on delivery.

  • A cash-on-delivery order is already on its way
  • Several unshipped orders — merge them into one parcel
  • The same device ordered under another name
  • Re-checked every 15 minutes — turns green on its own

A known refuser.

Payment quietly disappears. The buyer simply can't complete the order — and never learns they were blocked.

  • Matched by e-mail, phone, name + address or device
  • No payment at all, or only no cash on delivery — your rule
  • Optional automatic cancellation

02 · Device recognition

New name. Same device. Caught.

Refusers come back with a husband's e-mail, a sister's phone or a new address. Semafor recognises the device and browser they order from and links every identity into one person.

  • Blocked at checkout tooA new e-mail or phone typed on a blacklisted browser is stopped before the order exists.
  • Linked and countedOrders from one browser under different names over 90 days show up together, with their count, everywhere.
  • Installed with the appNo code. For full matching at checkout, switch on the Semafor app embed in your theme editor — one click.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to set anything up?

No. Install Semafor and it starts checking orders right away. Checkout blocking switches on automatically. Turn on the Semafor app embed in your theme editor once — it powers device recognition and the pop-up. No code.

Will a blocked customer know?

No. They simply see no payment method (or no cash on delivery), depending on the rule you chose. There is no message saying they were blocked.

What about privacy?

The store network only exchanges anonymous fingerprints, never names or addresses. Every e-mail has a one-click unsubscribe, and Shopify's data-deletion requests are applied automatically.

Do I need an e-mail service?

No. Reminder and welcome e-mails are sent by Semafor and included in your plan. If you prefer, connect your own domain with a step-by-step guide.

Which languages does Semafor speak?

The app speaks English, Romanian, Spanish, French, Polish and Russian. E-mails and the pop-up reach customers in English, Romanian, German, Polish or Spanish.

What happens after the free trial?

Your store moves to the Free plan and keeps checking 50 orders a month. Your settings are saved — choose a plan any time to switch everything back on.
